The protocol

Your asset stays
where it already is.

Vinculum Finalis is a permanently immutable, multi-chain commitment protocol. A user creates a Commitment Vault Lock using an approved asset on its supported source blockchain and receives a calculated quantity of VCLM on Base.

The source asset remains on its original blockchain for the entire lock. Nothing is bridged. Nothing is wrapped. Nothing is held in custody by anyone.

1,001 Approved assets
17 Supported environments
How it works Four steps
01

Lock on your own chain

Choose an approved asset, one of sixteen permitted durations, and where the principal returns at maturity. The fee is taken at creation and the remainder is isolated in its own contract.

02

Wait for finality

Each environment has its own deterministic finality condition. No relayer and no person can shorten or waive it.

03

Receive on Base

Base verifies the source event, then issues the complete calculated output to the recipient bound when the lock was created. Issuance is not immediate.

04

Reclaim at maturity

Release is user-initiated and requires no oracle, no relayer, no epoch processing, and no administrator. It works when nothing else does.
